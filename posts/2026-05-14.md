## 🔴 1. Notion turns its workspace into a hub for AI agents
**Category:** AI / Productivity Software  
**Impact:** Medium

**Why it matters:** It shows that the next AI battle is shifting from standalone assistants to platforms that connect agents, data, and internal workflows.

**What to know:** TechCrunch reported that Notion launched a developer platform with cloud-based Workers, database sync, and ways to connect both external and internal AI agents directly into Notion workflows. For teams, that could make Notion more like operational infrastructure than a notes app, but readers should separate the launch announcement from proof of broad adoption.
