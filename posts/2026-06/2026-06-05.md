# Daily Tech Digest - June 5, 2026

> **Today's signal:** The AI race is widening from models into capital, data centers, privacy tooling, and new interface ideas.

## AirTrunk commits $30B to AI data centers in India

AirTrunk said it will invest $30 billion in India by 2030 to develop 5 gigawatts of new data-center capacity. The move adds India to the growing list of regions competing for AI infrastructure investment.

Why it matters: AI capacity is becoming a geography story. Countries with power, land, cloud demand, and policy support are now competing to host the next wave of compute.

Source: [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/06/05/airtrunk-commits-30b-to-build-5gw-of-ai-data-centers-in-india/)

## Filtr uses Apple's URL filters to block ads beyond the browser

Filtr, built by the developer behind Wipr, uses new iOS 26 and macOS 26 URL-filtering features to block ads across iPhones, iPads, and Macs, not just inside Safari. The tool focuses on reducing tracking from ad networks at the device level.

Why it matters: privacy tools are moving closer to the operating system. If device-level filtering becomes common, app advertising and tracking could face pressure similar to what browser ad blockers created on the web.

Source: [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/06/04/filtr-is-a-new-privacy-tool-that-blocks-ads-in-almost-every-iphone-and-mac-app/)

## Anthropic's IPO path keeps AI return questions in focus

Anthropic co-founder Daniela Amodei defended the company's path toward a public listing, saying frontier AI companies need access to large amounts of capital for model training and inference. She also argued that businesses are still early in learning how to get value from AI.

Why it matters: the biggest AI companies are moving from private-market hype toward public-market scrutiny. Investors will increasingly ask whether massive compute spending can produce durable returns.

Source: [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/06/04/ahead-of-its-ipo-anthropics-daniela-amodei-shrugs-off-doubts-about-ais-returns/)

## Mira Murati previews continuous interaction models

Mira Murati's Thinking Machines Lab described work on "interaction models" that process continuous streams of audio, text, and video in short intervals instead of relying only on turn-by-turn prompting. The idea is to make AI interfaces feel closer to real-time human communication.

Why it matters: the next AI interface may not look like a chat box. Continuous multimodal systems could change how people collaborate with assistants, especially in creative, meeting, tutoring, and coding workflows.

Source: [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/06/04/mira-murati-steps-back-into-the-spotlight-carefully/)

June 5 showed how broad the AI market has become. The same daily cycle now includes billion-dollar infrastructure, operating-system privacy features, IPO readiness, and new ideas for real-time AI interaction.
