# Daily Tech Digest - June 3, 2026

> **Today's signal:** AI is becoming less of a standalone product story and more of an operating layer that changes infrastructure, security, and software-team economics.

## Coralogix raises $200M to monitor AI agents

Coralogix raised $200 million as it pushes deeper into observability for AI agents and agent-heavy software systems. The important shift is that AI adoption is creating a new monitoring problem: companies need to know what agents did, why they did it, and where automated workflows failed.

Why it matters: as agents move from demos into production, logging and dashboards are not enough. Teams will need monitoring that can follow decisions across prompts, tools, APIs, and human handoffs.

Source: [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/06/03/coralogix-raises-200m-in-race-to-build-the-monitoring-layer-for-ai-agents/)

## GitLab cuts staff while refocusing around AI workloads

GitLab cut 14% of its workforce while saying it is scaling its platform to serve AI workloads. The move shows how even developer-tool companies are reshaping their cost structures and product roadmaps around AI-assisted coding, automation, and platform demand.

Why it matters: AI is not just adding new products; it is changing how software companies allocate people, infrastructure, and investment.

Source: [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/06/03/gitlab-cuts-14-of-staff-as-it-scales-its-platform-to-serve-ai-workloads/)

## Instagram alerts users hit by AI chatbot account attacks

Instagram began alerting users who were targeted by hackers using attacks involving Meta's AI support chatbot. The report highlights a practical risk with AI support systems: if account recovery or moderation workflows are too easily steered, attackers can turn helpful automation into an account-takeover path.

Why it matters: AI support agents need security controls as strong as the human workflows they replace, especially when identity, access, or moderation decisions are involved.

Source: [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/06/03/instagram-is-alerting-users-who-were-targeted-by-hackers-during-ai-chatbot-attacks/)

## Quick Takeaway

June 3 was about operational reality. The strongest AI stories were not only new models, but the systems around them: monitoring agents, reorganizing developer platforms, and securing AI-mediated user support.
