# Daily Tech Digest - June 30, 2026

> **Today's signal:** Technology policy, product access, and supply-chain enforcement are converging around the data generated by phones and the chips that power AI.

## US Supreme Court limits geofence warrants

The court ruled that detailed cellphone location histories carry a reasonable expectation of privacy, limiting broad law-enforcement requests for devices found near a location.

**Why it matters:** Location databases can reveal sensitive movements at population scale, and the ruling strengthens constitutional protection for digital records.

Source: [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/06/29/in-major-privacy-win-supreme-court-rules-geofence-warrants-are-protected-by-privacy-rights/)

## Australia challenges Prime Video's ad changes

Australia's competition regulator sued Amazon over the addition of advertising to Prime Video and the extra fee required to remove it for existing subscribers.

**Why it matters:** Regulators are scrutinizing whether digital subscriptions can be materially changed after customers have already signed up.

Source: [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-06-29/amazon-sued-by-australian-consumer-watchdog-over-prime-video-ads)

## Gemini opens personalized image generation to more users

Google expanded personalized Nano Banana image generation in Gemini to eligible free users in the US, moving a previously paid capability into the broader product.

**Why it matters:** Advanced generative features are rapidly becoming baseline consumer expectations instead of premium-only tools.

Source: [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/06/29/geminis-personalized-ai-image-generation-is-now-free-for-u-s-users/)

## Taiwan expands an AI-chip smuggling investigation

Taiwanese authorities reportedly raided Super Micro's local office during an investigation into alleged Nvidia-chip smuggling to China.

**Why it matters:** Advanced-chip export rules now depend on enforcement across distributors, server makers, and international supply chains.

Source: [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-06-29/super-micro-office-raided-as-taiwan-expands-chip-smuggling-probe)

## Quick Takeaway

Digital rights and AI competition increasingly meet in the same place: who can collect data, change platform terms, and move scarce computing hardware.
