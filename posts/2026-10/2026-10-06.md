# Daily Tech Digest - October 6, 2026
> **Today's signal:** Users and developers are weighing AI convenience against control over data and compute.

## 🟡 1. LibreOffice says it will keep AI out of its default installation
**Category:** Open Source & Privacy  
**Impact:** Medium

**Why it matters:** The choice gives users a mainstream office suite whose core workflows do not send documents to an AI service. It also makes privacy and offline operation product features rather than optional settings.

**What to know:** The Document Foundation said it will not add AI to LibreOffice for the foreseeable future because current integrations do not meet its requirements for local data handling and provider independence. Users can still connect AI through extensions.

**Source:** [TechCrunch - LibreOffice says ‘no AI’ is now a software feature](https://techcrunch.com/2026/10/06/libreoffice-says-no-ai-is-now-a-software-feature/), October 6, 2026

## 🟡 2. Mistral introduced a model designed to compete at the top end
**Category:** Artificial Intelligence  
**Impact:** Medium

**Why it matters:** Large model releases keep pressure on both closed providers and open model developers. Real comparisons will depend on independent testing, operating costs, and access to model weights.

**What to know:** Mistral announced a new 1-trillion-parameter model and positioned it against leading closed and open systems. The model’s size makes efficient serving and the details of its release important parts of the competition.

**Source:** [TechCrunch - Mistral’s new 1T model aims to leapfrog closed and open rivals](https://techcrunch.com/2026/10/06/mistrals-new-1t-model-aims-to-leapfrog-closed-and-open-rivals/), October 6, 2026

**Quick Takeaway:** “AI included” and “largest model” are not automatic advantages; users still need privacy, control, and independent performance evidence.
