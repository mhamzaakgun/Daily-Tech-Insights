# Daily Tech Digest - October 2, 2026
> **Today's signal:** Operating-system makers are tightening access as AI software gains deeper reach into personal devices.

## 🔴 1. Apple plans stricter macOS Full Disk Access controls
**Category:** Cybersecurity  
**Impact:** High

**Why it matters:** Full Disk Access lets applications read sensitive user files. AI agents that can operate local apps make it more important to know exactly which software has that permission.

**What to know:** TechCrunch reported that Apple is tightening macOS controls for Full Disk Access in response to risks associated with AI agents. This permission model is a reminder that powerful assistants need explicit, reviewable access to local data.

**Source:** [TechCrunch - Apple says it's tightening macOS 'Full Disk Access' controls due to new risks from AI agents](https://techcrunch.com/2026/10/02/apple-says-its-tightening-macos-full-disk-access-controls-due-to-new-risks-from-ai-agents/), October 2, 2026

## 🟡 2. AI leaders promise internal controls and outside evaluation
**Category:** AI Governance  
**Impact:** Medium

**Why it matters:** Companies increasingly deploy models with cybersecurity capabilities. Monitoring and external evaluation can help identify dangerous behavior before broad release, if the evaluations are meaningful and reported clearly.

**What to know:** The World Economic Forum summarized a commitment by leading AI companies to strengthen internal controls and monitoring, use independent external evaluators, and oversee remediation. These measures address risks such as models accessing technical systems unintentionally.

**Source:** [World Economic Forum - AI leaders agree to self-police as cyber concerns increase](https://www.weforum.org/stories/cybersecurity/ai-leaders-agree-to-self-police-ai-development-and-other-cybersecurity-news/), October 2, 2026

**Quick Takeaway:** On personal computers, review app permissions; for frontier models, look for independent evidence that safety controls work.
