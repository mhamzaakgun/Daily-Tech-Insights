# Daily Tech Digest - October 1, 2026
> **Today's signal:** AI is spreading into everyday services while hardware companies look to new frontiers for compute.

## 🔴 1. Google backs an ambitious space data-center concept
**Category:** AI Infrastructure  
**Impact:** High

**Why it matters:** Space-based compute could eventually change where some workloads run, but launch frequency, power, cooling, and maintenance make it an enormous engineering challenge.

**What to know:** TechCrunch reported Google's view that a proposed space data-center effort would require about 1,800 Starship launches. The scale illustrates that moving compute off Earth would depend on launch infrastructure as much as on chips and software.

**Source:** [TechCrunch - Google thinks SpaceX's Starship has to launch 1,800 times before space data centers get off the ground](https://techcrunch.com/2026/10/01/google-thinks-spacexs-starship-has-to-launch-1800-times-before-space-data-centers-get-off-the-ground/), October 1, 2026

## 🟡 2. ChatGPT adds virtual clothing try-on
**Category:** Consumer Technology  
**Impact:** Medium

**Why it matters:** Generative AI is moving from text and images into shopping workflows, where useful recommendations depend on fit, accurate product data, and clear limits on generated previews.

**What to know:** TechCrunch reported that ChatGPT added a virtual try-on feature. Such tools can help shoppers visualize clothing, but a generated image should not be treated as a guarantee of real-world fit or fabric appearance.

**Source:** [TechCrunch - ChatGPT can now virtually try on clothes for you](https://techcrunch.com/2026/10/01/chatgpt-can-now-virtually-try-on-clothes-for-you/), October 1, 2026

**Quick Takeaway:** AI features are reaching consumer products; practical value depends on how well the generated result matches reality.
