# Daily Tech Digest - October 11, 2026
> **Today's signal:** A physical attack on cloud infrastructure showed how concentrated digital services can create shared points of failure.

## 🔴 1. A third Yandex data center was shut after a drone attack
**Category:** Cloud Infrastructure & Resilience  
**Impact:** High

**Why it matters:** Data centers host services used by many apps and businesses, so damage to one facility can affect far more than the operator’s own products. Geographic redundancy and tested recovery plans matter when infrastructure is physically disrupted.

**What to know:** Yandex said an attack overnight on October 11 damaged its Vladimir-region data center and forced it to stop operating. AFP reported service interruptions across several Yandex apps; the company said no employees were injured.

**Source:** [AFP - Drone hits Russia’s Yandex in third reported attack](https://www.afp.com/en/drone-hits-russias-yandex-third-reported-attack), October 11, 2026

**Quick Takeaway:** Cloud resilience depends on backup capacity and recovery plans that can withstand the loss of a real site.
