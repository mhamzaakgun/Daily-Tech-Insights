# Daily Tech Digest - July 10, 2026

> **Today's signal:** Regulators and investors both pushed tech companies toward accountability: Meta faced EU pressure over addictive design while AI startup dealmaking stayed unsettled.

## EU says Meta's addictive design may violate the DSA

The European Commission preliminarily found that Facebook and Instagram's addictive design could breach the Digital Services Act, focusing on features such as infinite scroll, autoplay, push notifications, and highly personalized recommender systems.

Why it matters: platform design is becoming a direct regulatory target. The EU is not only policing content moderation; it is also challenging the mechanics that keep users scrolling.

Sources: [European Commission](https://ec.europa.eu/), [The Verge](https://www.theverge.com/), [Techmeme archive](https://www.techmeme.com/260710/p1)

## Meta faces the possibility of forced product redesign

Reports from Reuters, CNBC, The Guardian, and others noted that Meta may need to change Facebook and Instagram or risk major fines if the preliminary findings stand.

Why it matters: fines hurt, but forced product changes can be more important. If regulators can make engagement-maximizing features illegal or risky, social platforms may need to rethink core growth loops.

Sources: [Reuters](https://www.reuters.com/), [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/), [Techmeme archive](https://www.techmeme.com/260710/p1)

## Manus deal talks show AI valuations are still fragile

Financial Times reporting said Manus investors and management were discussing unwinding Meta's proposed buyout, with Tencent in talks to become a major investor instead.

Why it matters: AI startup valuations can look enormous, but regulatory, strategic, and investor concerns can still reshape deals quickly. The talent and product race is tied to a messy financing market.

Source: [Techmeme archive](https://www.techmeme.com/260710/p1)

## Quick Takeaway

July 10 linked product design and company strategy. Platforms are being told to redesign for safety, while AI deals are being renegotiated under pressure from politics, valuation, and control.
