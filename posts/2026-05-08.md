## 🔴 1. Anthropic pushes AI agents toward self-improvement

**Category:** AI Agents  
**Impact:** High

**Why it matters:** AI agents are moving from simple task execution toward systems that can evaluate their own past behavior and improve future work.

**Student angle:** Anthropic introduced a "dreaming" capability for Claude Managed Agents, designed to let agents review previous sessions, identify useful patterns, and improve how they handle future tasks. For computer engineering students, this is a useful signal: agent design is becoming less about one prompt and more about memory, evaluation, orchestration, and long-running workflows.

**Source:** [VentureBeat - Anthropic introduces "dreaming"](https://venturebeat.com/technology/anthropic-introduces-dreaming-a-system-that-lets-ai-agents-learn-from-their-own-mistakes)

## 🔴 6. Apple and Intel reportedly reach a preliminary foundry agreement

**Category:** Semiconductors / Supply Chain  
**Impact:** High

**Why it matters:** Apple may be preparing to diversify advanced chip manufacturing beyond TSMC, while Intel could gain a major outside customer for its foundry business.

**What we know:** Reuters reported that The Wall Street Journal says Apple and Intel reached a preliminary chip-making agreement after more than a year of talks, but Apple and Intel have not officially confirmed the details. Earlier supply-chain reporting from Ming-Chi Kuo, covered by MacRumors, pointed to Intel potentially making Apple's lowest-end M-series chips around mid-2027 on Intel 18A. The safe reading is: this is a credible reported supply-chain move, not a confirmed product announcement.
