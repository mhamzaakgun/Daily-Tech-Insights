# Daily Tech Digest - August 7, 2026

> **Today's signal:** AI infrastructure spending held up even as investors questioned the durability of the boom.

## Apple commits deeper to domestic chip supply

Apple reportedly agreed to order more than $30 billion of Broadcom chips over five years, reinforcing the strategic value of U.S.-based semiconductor production.

**Why it matters:** Device makers are treating chip supply as a long-term resilience decision, not just a procurement line item.

## China raises security questions about Claude Code

Chinese authorities said they had identified security risks in Anthropic’s Claude Code as the U.S.–China AI competition moved deeper into developer tooling.

**Why it matters:** AI coding tools are becoming part of national-security debates because they can read repositories, execute commands, and influence software supply chains.

Source: [MarketScreener / Dow Jones](https://www.marketscreener.com/news/tech-up-as-ai-fears-subside-tech-roundup-ce7f5ed9dc8bf622)

## Meta plans another AI data center

Meta’s planned Alberta data center showed that the infrastructure race is spreading geographically as companies search for power, land, and more diversified capacity.

**Why it matters:** The AI platform race is increasingly constrained by physical infrastructure rather than model releases alone.
