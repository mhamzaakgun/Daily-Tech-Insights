# Daily Tech Digest - August 26, 2026

> **Today's signal:** AI moved deeper into infrastructure, from record chip demand and hyperscale GPU plans to browser-operating agents, while platform policy turned toward enforceable protections for younger users.

## NVIDIA's AI infrastructure revenue more than doubled
NVIDIA reported quarterly revenue of $96.2 billion, up 106% year over year, while Data Center revenue reached $89.0 billion, up 117%.

**Why it matters:** The results show that spending on AI compute is still accelerating, even as the industry debates whether the applications built on top of that infrastructure can justify the cost.

Source: [NVIDIA](https://nvidianews.nvidia.com/news/nvidia-announces-financial-results-for-second-quarter-fiscal-2027)

## AWS and NVIDIA planned two million additional GPUs
AWS and NVIDIA said they plan to deploy two million more Blackwell Ultra, Rubin, and Rubin Ultra GPUs across AWS infrastructure in 2027 and 2028, alongside new work on CPUs, networking, open models, and robotics.

**Why it matters:** Cloud competition is becoming a full-stack capacity race, and access to large GPU fleets will shape which companies can train and operate the next generation of AI systems.

Source: [Amazon](https://press.aboutamazon.com/aws/2026/8/aws-and-nvidia-to-deliver-2-million-additional-gpus-and-next-generation-infrastructure-for-agentic-and-physical-ai)

## Claude Cowork gained a separate built-in browser
Anthropic introduced a browser inside the Claude desktop app that can navigate sites, read pages, click, type, and fill forms without accessing a user's existing tabs, bookmarks, or passwords.

**Why it matters:** Giving agents a dedicated browser makes web automation easier to adopt, while the separation from a personal browser creates a clearer security boundary for credentials and active sessions.

Source: [Claude](https://claude.com/blog/cowork-built-in-browser)

## Meta agreed to new default protections for teens
Meta announced an agreement with 52 attorneys general that includes parental controls for changing daily limits, nighttime blocking, school-hour notification restrictions, age assurance, independent auditing, and research access.

**Why it matters:** The settlement pushes youth-safety features from optional product settings toward long-running, externally monitored requirements that may influence standards across major social platforms.

Source: [Meta](https://about.fb.com/news/2026/08/agreement-with-state-attorneys-general-supporting-teens/)

## Quick Takeaway
The day's developments showed AI scaling in two directions at once: more compute and more autonomous software, both accompanied by growing pressure for stronger operational and social safeguards.
