# Daily Tech Digest - August 25, 2026

> **Today's signal:** Specialized AI hardware moved into the spotlight while identity, developer ecosystems, and public digital infrastructure faced active security pressure.

## OpenAI published results from its Jalapeño inference chip
OpenAI said its first custom inference chip delivered strong throughput per kilowatt and low latency across multiple open model families.

**Why it matters:** Co-designing models, software, and silicon can reduce inference cost and energy use while limiting dependence on a single hardware supplier.

Source: [OpenAI](https://openai.com/index/the-full-stack-behind-abundant-intelligence/)

## Apple's new desktops targeted local AI development
Apple refreshed desktop hardware with configurations designed for large unified-memory workloads and multi-machine AI setups.

**Why it matters:** More capable local inference can improve privacy, lower cloud costs, and make experimentation accessible without a data center.

Source: [Ars Technica](https://arstechnica.com/apple/2026/08/apples-new-desktop-computers-are-designed-specifically-for-local-ai-development/)

## Attackers abused npm mirrors as phishing infrastructure
Researchers found malicious packages using npm and public mirrors to host fake Cloudflare verification pages that redirected victims to attacker-controlled sites.

**Why it matters:** Package ecosystems can be abused even without malicious installation code, so scanning must include hosted content and redirect behavior.

Source: [BleepingComputer](https://www.bleepingcomputer.com/news/security/hackers-abuse-npm-mirrors-to-host-phishing-redirect-pages/)

## WhatsApp added multiple passkeys and stronger two-step verification
WhatsApp began supporting separate passkeys across platforms and replaced its six-digit two-step PIN option with stronger alphanumeric passwords.

**Why it matters:** Phishing-resistant authentication and better recovery controls reduce the damage from stolen passwords and reused PINs.

Source: [BleepingComputer](https://www.bleepingcomputer.com/news/security/whatsapp-adds-stronger-two-step-verification-multiple-passkeys/)

## Quick Takeaway
The day's technology story connected both ends of the stack: purpose-built compute can accelerate AI, but strong identity and software-supply-chain controls keep that acceleration trustworthy.
