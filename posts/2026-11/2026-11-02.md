# Daily Tech Digest - November 2, 2026
> **Today's signal:** Connectivity investment and supply-chain security share the spotlight.

## 🔴 1. Marvell agrees to acquire Lumentum
Marvell and Lumentum announced an all-stock acquisition agreement with an implied enterprise value of $115 billion. The deal remains subject to closing conditions.

**Why it matters:** The proposed combination joins chip and optical technologies used to connect increasingly large computing systems.

**Source:** [Marvell - Acquisition announcement and SEC filing](https://investor.marvell.com/sec-filings/all-sec-filings/content/0001193125-26-442714/0001193125-26-442714.pdf), November 2, 2026

## 🟡 2. Partners disrupt Android TV botnet
Google and security partners working with Japanese police disrupted the XCPE botnet, which they linked to more than 1.3 million Android devices. Google attributed the backdoor operation to manufacturer Videostrong; the report says the company did not immediately respond.

**Why it matters:** Device supply-chain security matters because malicious firmware can expose home networks before users install anything.

**Source:** [The Register - Android TV botnet disruption](https://www.theregister.com/cyber-crime/2026/11/02/cops-bust-botnet-of-13m-infected-android-tv-boxes/5307045), November 2, 2026
