# Daily Tech Digest - May 15, 2026
> **Today's signal:** AI adoption is moving from experimentation toward governance and real-world rollout plans.

## 🔴 1. Google pushes AI implementation guidance for schools
**Category:** AI / Education Policy
**Impact:** Medium

**Why it matters:** Large-scale AI adoption depends on rules, training, and trust as much as model quality.

**What to know:** Google said it ran AI Policy & Guidance Labs across six countries to help education leaders turn broad AI ideas into 12-month implementation roadmaps. The practical signal is not a flashy product launch but a sign that institutions are now working on governance, teacher control, and deployment plans. Readers should treat this as a company-published update about an ongoing initiative, not independent proof that these programs will deliver strong classroom outcomes.

**Source:** [Google - From policy to practice: supporting the future of AI in education](https://blog.google/products-and-platforms/products/education/ai-policy-guidance-labs/), published May 15, 2026

**Quick Takeaway:** The next phase of AI competition is about who can make deployment usable and trusted in real institutions.
