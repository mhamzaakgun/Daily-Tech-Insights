## 🔴 1. Blockstream's Liquid Network halts after a major bitcoin withdrawal
**Category:** Blockchain / Security  
**Impact:** High

**Why it matters:** A custody or federation failure on a financial network can freeze transactions and undermine trust quickly.

**What to know:** Techmeme reported that Liquid Network stopped processing new transactions after purported white-hat hackers withdrew roughly 4,000 of the 4,200 bitcoin held in its federation wallet. The facts and intent behind the withdrawal were still being described as provisional in the reporting.

**Source:** [Techmeme - Blockstream's Liquid Network halted new transactions after a major bitcoin withdrawal](https://www.techmeme.com/260907/p1), published September 7, 2026

**Quick Takeaway:** One carefully verified development can be more useful than a longer list of weakly sourced claims.
