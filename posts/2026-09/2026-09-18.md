# Daily Tech Digest - September 18, 2026
> **Today's signal:** Frontier AI companies are moving from software-only research into laboratories where models can influence physical experiments.

## 🟠 1. Anthropic establishes a wet lab for AI-assisted biology
**Category:** AI and Biotechnology  
**Impact:** High

**Why it matters:** Connecting AI systems to real experiments could speed drug discovery, but it also raises the bar for laboratory safety, model oversight, and reproducibility.

**What to know:** Reuters reported that Anthropic created a Bay Area wet lab for physical biology work as it expands into disease-treatment research. The move gives the company a setting where model-generated ideas can be tested against biological evidence.

**Source:** [Reuters - Anthropic quietly sets up biology lab as it ramps AI drug program](https://www.reuters.com/world/anthropic-quietly-sets-up-biology-lab-it-ramps-ai-drug-program-2026-09-18/), published September 18, 2026

**Quick Takeaway:** The next phase of AI-for-science will be judged by experimental results, not benchmark scores alone.
