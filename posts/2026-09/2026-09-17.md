# Daily Tech Digest - September 17, 2026
> **Today's signal:** AI governance is beginning to borrow crisis-management ideas from nuclear diplomacy.

## 🟠 1. US and Chinese experts propose an AI incident hotline
**Category:** AI Policy  
**Impact:** High

**Why it matters:** A direct communication channel could reduce the risk that a serious AI-related event is misread as an intentional attack or escalates before experts compare evidence.

**What to know:** Reuters reported that US and Chinese experts proposed nuclear-style safeguards for advanced AI, including a dedicated military hotline, ahead of planned bilateral discussions. The proposal is an expert recommendation, not yet a binding agreement.

**Source:** [Reuters - US-China security experts propose nuclear-style safeguards for AI risks](https://www.reuters.com/world/china/us-china-security-experts-propose-nuclear-style-safeguards-ai-risks-2026-09-17/), published September 17, 2026

**Quick Takeaway:** Shared incident protocols may become as important as model rules when rival powers deploy increasingly capable AI systems.
