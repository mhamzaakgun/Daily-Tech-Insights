# Daily Tech Digest - September 21, 2026
> **Today's signal:** Humanoid robots have entered a measurable market, but research demand still outweighs proven industrial deployment.

## 🟡 1. Global humanoid robot sales reached about 7,000 units in 2025
**Category:** Robotics  
**Impact:** Medium

**Why it matters:** A real sales baseline makes it easier to separate rapid technical progress from exaggerated claims about near-term factory adoption.

**What to know:** Reuters reported International Federation of Robotics data showing roughly 7,000 autonomous humanoids were sold worldwide in 2025 for industrial and professional use. Many went to research programs rather than productive operations, limiting what the number says about commercial readiness.

**Source:** [Reuters - Humanoid robot sales tally hit 7,000 globally last year](https://www.reuters.com/technology/humanoid-robot-sales-tally-hit-7000-globally-last-year-2026-09-21/), published September 21, 2026

**Quick Takeaway:** Unit sales matter, but repeatable productive work is the stronger signal for humanoid robotics.
