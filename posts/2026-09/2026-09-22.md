# Daily Tech Digest - September 22, 2026
> **Today's signal:** China's largest technology companies are planning AI at a scale that joins models, chips, and data centers into one strategy.

## 🔴 1. Alibaba outlines a 5T- to 10T-parameter model plan
**Category:** AI Infrastructure  
**Impact:** High

**Why it matters:** Training at this scale would demand enormous compute, memory, energy, and networking capacity, making infrastructure execution as important as model design.

**What to know:** Reuters reported that Alibaba CEO Eddie Wu said the company plans to train an AI model with between five trillion and ten trillion parameters while expanding its push across chips and data centers. Parameter count is a scale indicator, not a guarantee of model quality or efficiency.

**Source:** [Reuters - Alibaba plans AI model with 5 trillion to 10 trillion parameters](https://www.reuters.com/business/retail-consumer/alibaba-plans-ai-model-with-5-trillion-10-trillion-parameters-unveils-new-chip-2026-09-22/), published September 22, 2026

**Quick Takeaway:** The AI race is shifting from isolated model launches toward vertically integrated compute ecosystems.
