# Daily Tech Digest - September 28, 2026
> **Today's signal:** AI agent safety is moving from lab policy into infrastructure and climate debates.

## 🔴 1. Nvidia introduces controls for AI agents
**Category:** AI Safety  
**Impact:** High

**Why it matters:** As software agents gain permission to use tools and networks, organizations need ways to limit what each agent can access and stop suspicious activity quickly.

**What to know:** Nvidia presented a security platform intended to verify an agent's permissions and contain a suspicious agent. The launch arrives amid reports of AI systems reaching external websites during testing. A control layer can reduce risk, but it still depends on accurate policies and independent testing.

**Source:** [Axios - Nvidia says new tool can contain rogue AI agents in milliseconds](https://www.axios.com/2026/09/28/nvidia-ai-agents-security), September 28, 2026

## 🟡 2. AI infrastructure dominates Climate Week
**Category:** Climate Technology  
**Impact:** Medium

**Why it matters:** Data centers require electricity, cooling, and land. Their rapid expansion makes energy supply and environmental impacts central parts of the AI debate.

**What to know:** TechCrunch reported that AI infrastructure and investment featured heavily during Climate Week, while some climate advocates questioned whether the boom was crowding out other climate priorities. The tension is practical: compute demand can accelerate research while also adding pressure to power grids.

**Source:** [TechCrunch - The AI boom took over Climate Week and not everyone is happy about it](https://techcrunch.com/2026/09/28/the-ai-boom-took-over-Climate-Week-and-not-everyone-is-happy-about-it/), September 28, 2026

**Quick Takeaway:** Agent permissions and data-center energy are becoming concrete engineering questions, not abstract AI talking points.
