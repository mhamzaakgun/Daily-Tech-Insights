## 🟡 1. Qualcomm and Apple extend their global licensing agreement
**Category:** Mobile Hardware  
**Impact:** Medium

**Why it matters:** The extension reduces a major legal and supply-chain uncertainty around technologies used across Apple's mobile products and Qualcomm's licensing business.

**What to know:** Reuters reported that Qualcomm extended its worldwide licensing agreement with Apple beginning in April 2027. The relationship grew out of the companies' 2019 settlement, although the new licensing extension does not by itself define every future modem-supply decision.

**Source:** [Reuters - Qualcomm and Apple extend licensing agreement](https://www.reuters.com/technology/qualcomm-apple-extend-licensing-agreement-chips-2026-09-24/), published September 24, 2026

**Quick Takeaway:** Patent licensing can remain durable even when hardware road maps and supplier relationships change.
