# Daily Tech Digest - September 26, 2026
> **Today's signal:** Enterprise software flaws can become mass-exploitation events when attackers automate discovery and access at scale.

## 🔴 1. Google warns of renewed mass exploitation of Oracle PeopleSoft
**Category:** Cybersecurity  
**Impact:** High

**Why it matters:** PeopleSoft often holds sensitive identity, finance, and workforce data, so broad exploitation can turn one software weakness into many organizational breaches.

**What to know:** Reuters reported that Google said the ShinyHunters group had renewed mass exploitation of an Oracle PeopleSoft flaw. The report underlines the need for affected organizations to combine patching with log review and credential rotation rather than treating remediation as a version-number check.

**Source:** [Reuters - ShinyHunters hackers expanded attacks on Oracle's PeopleSoft, Google says](https://www.reuters.com/legal/government/shinyhunters-hackers-expanded-attacks-oracles-peoplesoft-google-says-2026-09-26/), published September 26, 2026

**Quick Takeaway:** When exploitation is already widespread, patching is the start of incident response, not the end.
